Ultra adds real screen-space reflections on cars, roads, glass and mirrors, working rear-view and side mirrors, bloom, lens flare, clouds, 4K shadows and supersampling. It needs a strong GPU. Ray tracing (BETA, screen-space) ray-marches the picture to add glossy roads, stronger reflections on car paint, glass and water (real waves and boat wakes bend the reflections), strong global illumination (ambient occlusion, colour bounced from nearby surfaces, contact shadows), smoothed over time. It needs WebGL 2 and a real GPU (the button says Not supported otherwise), works in single player on High or Ultra, and switches itself off if your frame rate stays low. High and Ultra also get edge smoothing (FXAA), a sharpen filter, sun haze, colour grading for each time of day, bloom, and a night sky with stars and a moon. Voxel beams shows the soft-body skeleton and its damage. Destruction: Low = a simple 16-node car, Normal = 24 nodes, Realistic = 44 nodes, twice the polygons, a finer physics step, and parts that can tear off. Realistic Mode locks both to the maximum: 64-node cars, a much finer physics step, a gearbox, tyre load sensitivity, work-hardening metal, tearing parts, smoke and fire, clear-coat paint and headlights.
